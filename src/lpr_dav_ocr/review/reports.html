When regulars arrive

Time between repeat sightings

Gaps between sightings of the same plate on the same day. Under 2 minutes is usually one vehicle across adjacent clips.

Day-to-day overlap

Of the plates seen on the row day, the share also seen on the column day. The grey diagonal is a day against itself.

Plate type by day

Standard is three letters and four digits. TLC is the NYC taxi pattern. Other covers everything else.

State by sighting

Sightings whose plate has a state. A state you set on any sighting of a plate counts for all of them.

Watched plates

One row per watchlist entry, one dot per sighting. Rows show the nickname only.

Regulars

Plates seen on 2 or more days. Steady means 3 or more days with the first sighting inside a 90-minute window. Plate text is hidden until you tick "show plate text" here or at the top of the page.

Review effort by day

What happened to every read: labeled by you, approved or rejected automatically, or still waiting for review.

First-pass accuracy by hour

Share of your labeled real plates where the first read was already right. Columns with fewer than 15 plates are lighter.

All numbers are computed on this computer from your frames and labels. Plates you marked not a plate, obstructed or can't tell, and auto-rejected reads, are left out. A report of when named vehicles pass is a record of people's routines: see the privacy notes before sharing a screenshot.
